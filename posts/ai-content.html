How to Use AI Tools Without Losing Originality

Build a thoughtful workflow instead of publishing repetitive output.

Sample educational article · Update with your own research and examples before publishing

Begin with your own idea

Write down the specific problem your content will solve and the perspective you bring.

Use AI as an assistant

AI can help with outlining, research questions and editing, but verify factual claims and check usage rights.

Add meaningful human input

Include examples, original commentary, experiments or demonstrations from your own experience.

Review before publishing

Check accuracy, pacing, visuals, copyright and platform rules.

Keep improving

Use feedback and analytics to understand which parts of your work actually help viewers.
