YouTube Channel Audit: A Beginner’s Checklist

A practical starting point for reviewing your channel performance.

Sample educational article · Update with your own research and examples before publishing

Start with a clear goal

Decide whether you want more relevant viewers, better retention or more subscribers. Different goals require different analysis.

Review traffic sources

Open YouTube Studio and compare Search, Browse and Suggested traffic. Look for patterns rather than relying on one video.

Study click-through rate in context

A thumbnail and title should accurately describe the video. CTR varies widely with traffic source and audience, so avoid chasing a universal benchmark.

Check audience retention

Find where viewers leave and compare that moment with your introduction, pacing and delivery.

Create a simple action plan

Choose two improvements, test them on your next uploads and review the results.
